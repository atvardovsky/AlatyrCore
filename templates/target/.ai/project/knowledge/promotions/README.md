# Project Knowledge Promotion Records

Store reviewed promotion dispositions here when the target retention and
publication policy permits repository storage.

A promotion record links a reusable conclusion to engineering evidence,
repository evidence, a human decision, the canonical owner update, and the
derived route entry. It is decision evidence, not the canonical owner of the
accepted fact.

Rejected and deferred records may be retained when they prevent repeated
discussion, subject to the target retention and redaction policy.
