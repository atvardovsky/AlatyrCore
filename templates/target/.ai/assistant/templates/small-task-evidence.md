and no expansion trigger fired.

- Task classification: `small-task`
- Task decomposition: `one-node` with implementation level `{L0_L1_L2_L3_OR_L4}`
- Selected operation: `{SELECTED_OPERATION}`
- Selected profile: `{SELECTED_PROFILE}`

- Large-task packet: `not created - small-task`
- Change package: `not created - small-task`
- Focused validation: `{CHECKS_RUN_AND_RESULTS}`
- Broader checks skipped: `{SKIPPED_CHECKS_AND_REASON}`

contract, live-external, multi-area, failed-validation, or broad-audit triggers
appear, stop using this template and route through the broader selected
profile, overlay, approval, and evidence contracts.
