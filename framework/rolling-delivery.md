# Rolling Delivery

Conditional [large-task orchestration](large-task-orchestration.md) for explicitly
selected growing queues of independently deliverable work. Small tasks retain
their ordinary route. No additional registry, scheduler, mode or authority.

## Queue

Use stable item IDs and source-linked original outcomes in existing operation,
model and worker evidence. The primary owns priority, arrival order, assignments,
review and convergence. Team records retain applicable claims. Grouping must
not lose outcomes or create permissions. Worker completion, primary local
acceptance, validation, publication and final outcome acceptance remain distinct.
Local `DONE` proves neither deployment nor runtime health.

## Checkpoints

Reconsider after returns, accepted review, resource release, changed prerequisites,
new requests or material source changes. Review before refill: returned results
retain scope/resource claims and review capacity. Use canonical admission,
resource and readiness resolvers; only accepted prerequisites unlock dependents.
Respect delegation preferences and cumulative worker/context/result/retry/review
limits; waves reset none. Keep primary review/integration on the critical path.
Record a concrete next decision or hold. Independent authorized work may continue
despite unrelated blockers; workers cannot self-admit new scope.

## Waves

When authorized, freeze compatible primary-accepted local results for an available
validation lane; one item suffices. Preserve atomic/per-item release instructions.
Later arrivals stay outside the wave; independent work stays outside its candidate
lane. Retain ordered membership, owners, request/outcome mappings, material-input
hashes and checkpoints. Changed bytes/assignments require reconciliation and
affected proof. Removed failures form a new candidate requiring qualification.
Validate combined effects, per-item checks and project-contour companions.
Hashes establish identity, not input completeness, semantic compatibility,
external-state freshness, authorization or execution; canonical evidence owns these.

## Barriers

Bind "after everything" to an explicit prerequisite snapshot; reconcile arrivals
rather than silently extending/bypassing it. Open-ended barriers may remain pending.
The helper holds final-barrier dispatch for coordinator review, even after local
acceptance. Classify each new request's scope/action phases: prior publication/live
approval does not transfer. Status questions alone do not close unfinished work;
newest intent, pauses, cancellation, redirection and stale bindings win. Client
suspension may end execution; instructions guarantee no background continuation.

## Limits

Opt-in schema 2 retains schema-1 selection, adding bound briefs, exact outcome
coverage, frozen inputs and barriers. Cooperation rechecks sources through output;
peer uncertainty suppresses proposals. Views advise only; overflow returns counts
and references, not truncated actionable constraints. Preserve schema-1 packets
and defaults during migration; never infer missing bindings from narratives.
Without tooling, disclose manual verification. Unsupported clients use permitted
sequential fallback or report required-delegation blockers. Installation enables
neither this style, workers nor hooks. Measure wait, execution, review, rework,
context and cost separately; structural tests prove no general savings or compliance.
